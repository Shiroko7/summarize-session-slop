# RPG Session Summary

## **Session Overview**  
The party, led by Son Taewoo, is navigating a dangerous dungeon in the **Forbidden Zone** to eliminate a plague. They encounter a mysterious cult of Cyric, uncover ancient secrets tied to **Sirius**, and face a confrontation with a **dragon** and its cultists. The group splits to explore, discovers a **tomb** containing a map, and experiences visions of past events. The session culminates in a battle against a **dragon** and its followers, with the party uncovering a dark history involving **Vasco**, **Sirius**, and a ritual that binds their fates.

---

## **Locations Visited**  
- **Forbidden Zone**: A dangerous area with collapsing floors and hidden traps.  
- **Tomb of Vasco**: A crypt containing a **map** and visions of ancient battles.  
- **Caverns of the Cult**: A location where the cult of Cyric is active, with hidden passages and traps.  
- **Dragon's Lair**: A chamber housing a **white dragon** and its followers, tied to the cult's rituals.  

---

## **NPCs Met/Interacted With**  
- **Kanjun**: A follower of Cyric who reveals the cult's plan to sacrifice followers to resurrect Vasco's wife.  
- **Son Taewoo**: The party's guide, who initially brought them to the dungeon to eliminate a plague but later becomes entangled in the cult's schemes.  
- **Sirius**: A legendary figure whose death and resurrection are tied to the cult's rituals.  
- **Vasco**: A fallen hero whose tomb is central to the plot, with his spirit bound to the map.  

---

## **Key Plot Points & Lore**  
1. **Cult of Cyric**: The cult seeks to resurrect Vasco's wife by sacrificing followers, using a ritual tied to a **dragon's power**.  
2. **Sirius's Legacy**: Sirius, a fallen hero, was betrayed by Vasco and his wife, leading to a tragic ritual that binds their fates.  
3. **The Map**: A cursed artifact that reveals visions of past battles, including Sirius's fight against a **dragon** and the creation of a **virus** that spread across the continent.  
4. **Vasco's Tomb**: The tomb contains a **dragon's essence**, which the cult seeks to harness. The party discovers Vasco's spirit is trapped, bound to the map.  
5. **Dragon's Ritual**: The cult plans to use the dragon's power to resurrect Vasco's wife, but the ritual requires a **sacrifice** (likely the party members).  

---

## **Loot & Items**  
- **Map of the Tomb**: A cursed artifact that reveals visions of ancient battles and hidden locations.  
- **Potion of Bone Destruction**: A potion created to counter the dragon's power, though its use is debated.  
- **Dragon's Essence**: A fragment of the dragon's power found in Vasco's tomb, tied to the cult's rituals.  

---

## **Combat Outcomes**  
- **Battle with the Dragon**: The party confronts a **white dragon** in its lair. Using the map's visions and strategic positioning, they manage to weaken the dragon but are forced to retreat due to overwhelming odds.  
- **Cultists' Ambush**: The cult of Cyric ambushes the party in the caverns, forcing a chaotic fight where the party uses **spells**, **traps**, and **melee attacks** to survive.  
- **Final Confrontation**: A surprise round allows the party to strike first, dealing critical damage to the cult's leader and forcing them to retreat.  

--- 

This session deepens the lore of the **Forbidden Zone**, ties the party's actions to the **legacy of Sirius and Vasco**, and sets up future conflicts with the cult of Cyric and the dragon's power.

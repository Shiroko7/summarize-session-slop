# Session Summary

## **Session Overview**  
The party, led by Son Taewoo, is navigating a dangerous dungeon to eliminate a threat. They uncover a conspiracy involving Sirik, an archovispo, and a ritual to contain a curse. A mysterious figure (Minjun) reveals that they were sacrificed by Sirik's followers and that Santa Eon is manipulating events to sacrifice hybrid beings. The group debates whether to trust Minjun, regroup, and seek answers from Santa Yú, while also exploring a cryptic map that reveals ancient history tied to a dragon and a curse.

---

## **Locations Visited**  
- **The Forbidden Zone**: A dungeon filled with traps, undead, and ancient secrets.  
- **The Cryptic Chamber**: Contains a cadaver-shaped map that unlocks visions of the past.  
- **The Tomb of Vascolt**: A resting place of a key figure tied to the curse and the ritual.  
- **The Shrine of the Dragon**: A location where ancient battles and rituals occurred.  

---

## **NPCs Met/Interacted With**  
- **Son Taewoo**: The party's guide and former ally, now trapped in the dungeon.  
- **Minjun**: A mysterious figure claiming to be a follower of Sirik, who reveals the party's sacrifice and Santa Eon's machinations.  
- **Santa Yú**: A central figure suspected of orchestrating the ritual to contain a curse.  
- **Sirius (implied)**: A legendary figure tied to the curse, whose history is revealed through visions.  
- **Vascolt**: A sacrificed figure who protected Sirius and the princess, whose tomb is explored.  

---

## **Key Plot Points & Lore**  
1. **The Curse of the Dragon**: A curse tied to a dragon's power, which caused catastrophic events in the past.  
2. **The Ritual**: A ritual performed by Sirius and the princess to contain the curse, resulting in a "mark" that spreads like a virus.  
3. **Sirik and the Archovispo**: A cult led by Sirik seeks to sacrifice hybrid beings to gain power, with Santa Eon as a manipulative figure.  
4. **The Visions**: The map unlocks visions of a battle between Sirius, a dragon, and shadowy figures, revealing a complex web of alliances and betrayals.  
5. **The Sacrifice**: Vascolt, a key figure, sacrificed himself to protect Sirius and the princess, with his tomb now a focal point of the plot.  

---

## **Loot & Items**  
- **A Potion**: A rare elixir crafted to counter the power of judges, though its use remains uncertain.  
- **The Map**: A cadaver-shaped artifact that reveals hidden lore and triggers visions.  
- **Ancient Relics**: Items found in the tomb, including a key that interacts with the map.  
- **Weapons and Armor**: Recovered from various locations, including a corrupted armadura that requires careful handling.  

---

## **Combat Outcomes**  
- **Encounter with a Dragon**: A battle against a white dragon, which resulted in visions of the past and a near-fatal trap.  
- **Trap Activation**: The map triggered a series of illusions and traps, including a leek-like projectile and a collapsing chamber.  
- **Ritual Combat**: A confrontation involving the dragon's energy and the group's attempts to stabilize the curse's effects.  
- **Survival**: The party narrowly escaped collapsing structures and deadly traps, with injuries sustained by Kimzora and others.  

--- 

This session deepened the lore of the curse, the cult's motives, and the party's role in a larger conflict, while introducing new threats and alliances.
